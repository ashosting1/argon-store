Store Rules

Follow these guidelines to ensure everyone has a great experience.

Prohibited Content

  • No NSFW or inappropriate content
  • No hate speech, racism, or discrimination
  • No spam or excessive self-promotion
  • No harassment or personal attacks

Recommended Behavior

  • Be respectful to all members
  • Keep discussions in appropriate channels
  • Use English as the primary language
  • Follow Discord's Terms of Service

Violations may result in warnings, mutes, or bans depending on severity. Staff decisions are final.
